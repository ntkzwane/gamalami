About Ditema tsa Dinoko

Vowel System (Onkamisa)

  • i → △ (upward triangle)
  • a → ▽ (downward triangle)
  • u → ◁ (left-pointing triangle)
  • o → ▷ (right-pointing triangle)
  • e → varies by language

Consonant Principles

  • Straight lines = plosives (p, t, k)
  • Curved lines = fricatives (f, s, x)
  • Circles = nasals (m, n, ng)
  • Position = place of articulation
